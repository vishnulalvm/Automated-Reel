Section: Basics
Episode: 2
Title: Your first prompt
Next: Giving Claude context about you and your situation, so answers fit you

Story:
- Most people's first prompt is a few words, like "write an email". Claude then has to guess the rest.
- Better: brief Claude like a brilliant new teammate who knows nothing about your situation.
- Tell it three things: who it is for, what you want, and what good looks like.
- Example: "Write a short, friendly email to my landlord, asking to fix the heater this week."
- Takeaway: clear beats clever.
